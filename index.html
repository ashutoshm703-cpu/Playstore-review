Tier 1: When to Show the Micro-Rating Popup

Activity Completion Criteria

ActivityCompleted WhenNOT Completed
Mock Test / Live Test Submitted (manual or auto on timer) + at least 25% of questions attempted + student stayed for at least 40% of test duration App closed without submission. Fewer than 25% questions attempted. Spent less than 40% of allotted time (e.g., submitted a 60-min test in under 24 min with few answers).
Video Lecture 80% of duration actually played (not seek position). 2x speed OK. Scrubbed to 80% without watching. Paused before 80%.
Question Bank 20+ questions answered in single session Fewer than 20 questions.
Score does NOT affect completion. A student who scores 15% has their activity completed, same as 95%.

The Prompting Engine

One sentence: Every 3rd eligible activity, but never more than once per 5 days.

N = 3 (always) N = 6 (after negative response, next cycle only, then back to 3) Minimum gap = 5 calendar days since last prompt Lifetime cap = 15 ratings Session cap = 1 per session Inactivity reset = 3+ consecutive days → counter AND gap timer both reset

How the counter works

Activity 1 → counter = 1 Activity 2 → counter = 2 Activity 3 → counter = 3 → CHECK: 5 days since last prompt? → YES → PROMPT → counter resets to 0 → NO → counter stays at 3, waits for gap to clear
The counter resets to 0 after each prompt (whether rated or skipped). It does not keep incrementing. It always counts to 3 (or 6 after negative), fires, resets.

The 5-day gap

RuleHow It Works
Calculationtoday's date - last_prompt_date >= 5. Calendar days, not timestamps. No time-of-day tracking.
ExamplePrompt shown on April 10 → next prompt eligible on April 15 or later (any time that day).
Why 5 daysNaturally limits to ~5-6 prompts/month. Evenly spaced. No front-loading.

What this looks like in practice

Active Student (3/day)Free Student (1/day)
Prompt 1Day 1 (counter hits 3 on day 1)Day 3 (counter hits 3)
Prompt 2Day 6 (counter hit 3 on day 2, waited for 5-day gap)Day 8 (counter hits 3, gap clear)
Prompt 3Day 11Day 13
Prompt 4Day 16Day 18
Month total~5-6 prompts, evenly spaced~4 prompts, evenly spaced

Safety Layers

5-Day Minimum Gap

Counter must reach N AND at least 5 calendar days since last prompt. Prevents front-loading. No monthly cap needed.

Negative Backoff

After a negative response, N doubles to 6 for the next cycle only. After the next positive/neutral/skip, N returns to 3.

Lifetime Retirement

Micro-ratings stop permanently when EITHER: (a) 15 total ratings given, OR (b) student completes a Play Store review. Whichever comes first.

Tier 2: When to Show "Rate Us on Play Store"

9 Eligibility Gates

#GateDefaultWhy
1Days since install >= 77Not brand new
2Total sessions >= 55Consistent engagement
3At least 1 micro-rating given1We have signal
4Last micro-rating was positive—Current sentiment is good
5No active support ticket—Not mid-complaint
6Happiness moment just occurred—Right emotional timing
7Play Store ask not shown in last 90 days90Platform quota compliance
8Lifetime Play Store asks < 22Don't over-ask
9play_store_ask_enabled = truetrueKill switch
Same gates for free and paid users. Free users naturally reach eligibility with fewer activities — the system self-adjusts.

Happiness Moments

All happiness moments require Gate 4 (last micro-rating was positive) to be true. A happiness moment alone does not trigger the Play Store ask — the student must also have positive recent sentiment.
MomentTriggerFree User?Paid User?
Test score >= 60%Any mock test or live test completed with score >= 60% (first test, tenth test — doesn't matter which)YesYes
5th video completedLifetime count of videos watched to 80%+ reaches 5. Only videos that pass the 80% watch-time threshold count.Yes (free videos)Yes
Strong Q-bank session70%+ accuracy in the last 20 questions answeredYesYes

Collision Prevention (Micro-Rating + Play Store in Same Session)

SituationWhat Happens
No collision: Happiness moment + gates pass + no micro-rating shown this session Show Play Store ask immediately. No waiting.
Collision: Happiness moment + gates pass + micro-rating WAS shown this session (at any point) Set play_store_deferred = true. Do NOT show Play Store ask. Wait for the next happiness moment in a future session.

Deferred Flow (when collision detected)

StepWhat HappensPlay Store Ask?
1. Collision detected in Session ASet play_store_deferred = true. Session continues normally.No
2. Session A endsStudent closes app or 30+ min inactivity.No
3. Session B (or later) beginsStudent uses app. System monitors for happiness moment.No
4. Happiness moment in new sessionRe-check ALL 9 gates fresh. Is last micro-rating still positive? No micro-rating shown this session?Depends on gate check
4a. All gates pass + no collisionShow Play Store ask.Yes
4b. Gates fail (e.g., last rating was negative)Cancel. play_store_deferred = false. Must re-qualify from scratch.No
4c. Another collision (micro-rating also shown this session)Defer again. Wait for next session's happiness moment.No
Why wait for a happiness moment, not just "first activity of next session"? Because Gate 4 only checks the last micro-rating — which could be stale. The student might have 3 bad experiences without being prompted for a micro-rating (counter not at N). A happiness moment is real-time proof the student is happy RIGHT NOW.
If the student never hits another happiness moment: The Play Store ask never fires. And that is correct — if they can't score 60% on a test or 70% on Q-bank, they are not having peak moments worth leveraging.

The Prompt

Enjoying NPrep?

A quick rating helps fellow nursing students find us.

Not now
On "Rate on Play Store" → trigger Google Play In-App Review API. The native Play Store card appears. We do NOT know what rating the student gives — API doesn't return this.
After Play Store API triggered → RETIRE THE ENTIRE REVIEW SYSTEM. Set micro_rating_retired = true. No more micro-rating popups. No more Play Store asks. The student's journey is complete. Automatic backend support triggers stay active.
On "Not now" → dismiss. Set cooldown 90 days. Increment lifetime count. Student may be asked once more (lifetime cap = 2).

Automatic Support Triggers

SignalActionPriority
Average micro-rating drops below 2.0 Auto-create support ticket. Team reaches out: "We noticed you're not having a great experience. How can we help?" High
2 consecutive negative ratings Same outreach trigger High
"Found a wrong answer" in Q-bank follow-up Auto-create content review ticket with question IDs Critical
3 consecutive skips Flag for review — student may be disengaged Low
Detractor detection is immediate and proactive — the student doesn't have to fill a survey. The system detects unhappiness from behavioral signals and acts.
